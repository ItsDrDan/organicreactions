📘 How to use this game Tap to open
  1. Pick a mode. Identify the Reaction, Draw the Product, Draw the Reactant, or Full Reaction (all three steps).
  2. Draw on the pad. With ✏️ Draw on, tap empty space to place an atom, tap an atom to grow a new bond from it, or drag from one atom to another to connect them. Tap a bond to cycle single → double → triple.
  3. Pick the element first (C, O, N, Cl, Br) for new atoms. Use 🔁 Swap to change an atom you already drew, and ± Charge for O⁻ or N⁺ ions.
  4. Hydrogens are automatic. Every corner and line end is a carbon. Hydrogens fill in to give C four bonds, N three and O two.
  5. Two products? Draw them as two separate molecules in the same box. You don't need to draw water or inorganic ions such as Na⁺ or Cl⁻.
  6. Earn XP and badges for independent answers and streaks. Hint gives a clue; Show Answer reveals the answer but earns no point.

Tip: Decode the name first: the stem gives the carbon count (meth 1, eth 2, prop 3, but 4, pent 5, hex 6), the ending gives the functional group, and the number tells you where it goes.
